# Knossos Field village-builder mockup v1

Mode: built-in image generation

Use case: high-fidelity desktop UI mockup for the Knossos Field agent-orchestration harness.

Create a wide 16:9 interface in which a Mediterranean strategic relief map fills almost the entire screen. The operator-selected Capital is the main project; four smaller settlements represent projects at different growth tiers. Infrastructure is expressed through settlement architecture rather than floating UI icons. Agents move between projects along geographic roads, collaboration routes are restrained, and red-team and blue-team positions face a contested project.

Use an original premium village-building strategy-game language crossed with a sophisticated Roman campaign atlas: chunky readable structures, satisfying upgrade tiers, carved limestone, terracotta, walnut, aged brass, oxblood cloth, and amber firelight. Make the world active with forge sparks, standards, smoke, lamps, scaffolding, and couriers.

Keep the interface restrained: a thin top rail with `CAMEO`, `WORLD`, `CAMPAIGN`, and `PROJECT`; one selected-agent parchment showing `MARCUS`, `Verify deployment`, and `62%`; a compact cohort strip at the bottom; and a tasteful minimap. The product should answer where agents are, whether they are running, who they are working with, and what they are working on.

Avoid Vatican imagery, medieval fantasy, cyberpunk, neon grids, generic AI symbols, floating icon tiles, glassmorphism, dashboard clutter, hard territory polygons, heavy red outlines, currency bars, copied game assets or branding, and excessive text.
