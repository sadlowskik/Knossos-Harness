# Knossos Field illustrated atlas mockup v2

Mode: built-in image generation, style-transfer edit of the v1 mockup.

Preserve the v1 product concept, major geography, capital, project settlements, agent routes, opposing positions, inspector, navigation, minimap, and cohort strip. Make the entire interface substantially less 3D.

Transform the world into a refined living campaign atlas: mostly top-down geography, gentle relief, hand-painted terrain, engraved cartographic detail, and shallow three-quarter architectural symbols. Integrate settlements into the map rather than placing toy-like dioramas on it. Use restrained shadows, flatter silhouettes, elegant roads and waterways, and small standards for agents. Derive depth from hierarchy, texture, atmosphere, and light rather than extruded geometry.

Retain the Cameo palette and Roman material language. Flatten the HUD into engraved bronze rails and parchment with restrained rules and shadows. Target roughly 75% illustrated 2D and 25% shallow dimensional detail.

Avoid changing the layout or adding cards, labels, dashboards, currency bars, glossy bevels, heavy 3D structures, miniature characters, neon, cyberpunk, glassmorphism, or copied game assets.
